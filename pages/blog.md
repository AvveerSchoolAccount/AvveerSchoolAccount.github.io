“The measure of intelligence is the ability to change.” — Albert Einstein

Intelligence isn’t just knowing things, but adapting when new evidence or better ideas appear.


“The important thing is not to stop questioning.” — Albert Einstein

Progress in science and technology comes from continuous curiosity, not fixed answers.


“Smart people learn from everything and everyone.” — Socrates

Insight often comes from unexpected sources, not just formal expertise.


“Real knowledge is to know the extent of one’s ignorance.” — Confucius

Understanding what you don’t know is the foundation of meaningful learning.


“An expert is someone who knows some of the worst mistakes that can be made in their subject, and how to avoid them.” — Werner Heisenberg

Expertise is built through experience, failure, and reflection—not perfection.
